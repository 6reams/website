## Problem

Mentoring 40+ students across 10 CTF teams meant everyone needed somewhere safe and consistent to
practise — isolated, resettable, and realistic — without each student having to configure a lab
from scratch.

## Approach

- Containerised a set of deliberately vulnerable challenges so each one spins up clean and isolated.
- Grouped challenges by category (web, Active Directory, forensics, crypto) matching the
  competition formats students were preparing for.
- Wrote hands-on labs and walkthroughs that build from fundamentals to full exploitation, so
  beginners and advanced students could use the same platform.

## Result

A repeatable training environment used to prepare teams for competition. Replace this with the real
numbers and outcomes from your own work — placements, number of students, challenges built.
